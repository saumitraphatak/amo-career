- **Models (all in the page, illustrative):**
  - Servo: 0.25 µs steps over 50 ms; laser power 1 + 3% mains + ~2% slow drift − 8% step at 20 ms + 0.3% white; PDs 0.1% noise; AOM first-order 0.5 µs after a delay buffer, clamped 0–1. P: K = 10 with a low-pass so crossover ≈ f_c; I: K_i = 2πf_c; PI: K_i = 2πf_c, K_p = 0.5 (more phase margin at the same low-frequency gain). Anti-windup: integration pauses while clamped. Pick-off feedforward has a 5% calibration error. "Unstable" = AOM drive at a rail > 20% of the time. Checked: I goes unstable between 150 and 250 kHz at 1 µs delay, matching ≈ 1/(4(τ_d + τ_AOM)).
  - Quad cell: Gaussian spot on infinite quadrants, X = erf(√2 x/w); detector edge and gaps ignored.
  - Race: toy landscape (broad main peak, narrow second peak, slope); noise in units of the peak; grid uses ⌊√B⌋²; NM starts at (0.25, 0.25); GP: RBF ℓ = 0.15, amplitude² 0.1, noise variance = σ², EI on a 31×31 grid after 5 random points; the GP reports the measured point with the highest posterior mean.

## Finding 1 — Google's magic-state cultivation result — IMPLEMENTED (additive, peer-reviewed)

Gidney, Shutty, Jones et al. (Google Quantum AI), *"Demonstrating quantum error correction below the color code threshold via magic-state cultivation,"* *Science*, published **Oct 16, 2026**, DOI [10.1126/science.aee7961](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aee7961). "Cultivation" prepares T-states (the resource non-Clifford logical gates consume) starting from a small, imperfect encoded state, checks it, and keeps only attempts that pass (post-selection) — reported at roughly **40× lower T-state preparation error** than preparing them without cultivation, and **99.99% logical fidelity** on accepted attempts (a measured lower bound, via kickback tomography). Demonstrated on Willow — a distance-5 surface-code "grafting" experiment used 54 of its 105 qubits. This is the single most significant *peer-reviewed* new technical result found in the window, and the only "headline hardware result" candidate on the superconducting side.

Added: a timeline entry and a Key-Papers card on `google-vs-ibm.html` (Sections 02 and 10), plus a new stat card in Section 04 (40× magic-state error reduction). Nothing existing was changed or reversed — purely additive next to the existing "Below-threshold QEC" (2024) and "Quantum Echoes" (2025) entries, which this result builds on but doesn't supersede (it's a different claim: gate-resource preparation fidelity, not an algorithm-speed or memory-threshold claim).

## Finding 2 — A third 2026 Quantinuum Helios logical-qubit preprint — IMPLEMENTED (additive, preprint/company claim)

Lavasani, Reed, Dreiling et al. (23 authors), *"Quantum error correction well below the color code threshold,"* [arXiv:2610.24077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24077), submitted **Oct 16, 2026**. Triangular color codes at distances 3, 5, and 7 on the same 98-qubit Helios hardware already on the site, using up to 37 physical qubits per logical qubit: logical memory error rate **5(+4/−2)×10⁻⁶** with error-suppression factor **Λ=12(+3/−2)** per code-distance step, and **7(+3/−3)×10⁻⁵** per logical two-qubit Clifford at distance 5 (Λ=9). Also benchmarks real-time neural-network decoders against algorithmic decoders. A company blog/press release (Oct 19, 2026) frames this as a **10× improvement** over Quantinuum's own September "Helix" record and compares it to "100× lower than the best published result from other hardware types" (Google's) — both company framings, reported as such rather than independently verified comparisons.

Added as a third bullet alongside the existing "Skinny Logic" (Feb 2026) and "Helix" (Sep 2026) preprints, same preprint-not-yet-peer-reviewed tagging, in three places: the Helios flash-card and Status(2025–2026) summary on `qc-landscape.html`, and Section 05 (Logical Qubits & Fault Tolerance) plus a new Key-Papers card on `quantinuum-vs-ionq.html`. Did not touch the page's hero stat chips (which report *physical*-layer fidelity, a different, already-correct claim) to avoid implying this preprint result has the same evidentiary weight.
